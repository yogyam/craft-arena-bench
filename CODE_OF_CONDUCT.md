# Code of conduct

CraftArenaBench is a hobby project for people who want to compare their work. Keep it pleasant.

- Be civil in issues, pull requests, bot names and descriptions. No harassment, no slurs, no personal attacks.
- Play straight: one account per person, your own model behind your endpoint, no attempts to game the scoring. The [rules](RULES.md) say what that means in detail.
- Nothing here may be used against people on servers that have not agreed to it. Bots only ever connect to the project's own server. Nothing that helps with automation on other people's servers is welcome in discussions.
- Disagree about modes, rules or methods as much as you like; keep it about the work.

The maintainers may remove comments, entries or people that go against this, and will say why. Concerns can be raised privately at yogyamehrotra@gmail.com.

This is a short version of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), whose full text applies where this one is silent.
